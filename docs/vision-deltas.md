## 2026-06-20 (campaign C44) — The solitude principle: a world abandoned for a century, no signs of living human life

**Source:** user steering note (campaign inbox).

> "everything in the world should be a wreck/decayed for a hundred years… a watchtower still standing reads as recent and that people could be close by… i want the player to feel alone and have almost no signs of living human life, this goes for pois as well."

**Intent.** The desert must read as **abandoned for ~100 years** and the player must feel **utterly alone**. Anything that reads as *recent*, *maintained*, or *currently-inhabited* infrastructure breaks the tone — even a single "someone lives here" prop (a standing lookout, a working winch, a bucket on a rope) undoes the solitude the whole world is built for.

**Applies to all POIs and future structures**, including M7 ⑦ walkable-wreck-interiors and anything in M8–M10. Build wreckage and decay, not settlements or working machinery.

**First application (C44):** removed the `watchtower` archetype; re-scoped the `well` into a long-dry collapsed ruin (no bucket/rope/working crank). See [decisions.md](decisions.md) D252.
